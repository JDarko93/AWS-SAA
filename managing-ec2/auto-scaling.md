## AWS Auto Scaling
- Horizontally scaling in or scaling out is when you add or take away instances based on demand
- Automated capacity adjustment for required resources 
- Benefits are performance maintenance during busy peaks
- Scaling in app usage when demand is low can save costs as you are not running unessessary instances

### Auto scaling servies 
- EC2 Instances
- Elastic Container Service
- Amazon DynamoDB
- Amazon Aurora database replicas

### Predictive scaling
- Thhis applkies to EC2 auto scaling groups and can scale proactively based on historical activity patterns
