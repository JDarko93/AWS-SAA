## Amazon Macie 

- Macie is a cloud based managed service that goes through stored documents to see if there are any PII in them and point them out
- You can run a schedule job daily, weekly, monthly on specified resources like S3 buckets
- It can also do one time jobs if needed 
- You can specify what types of Regular Expression (REGEX) for macie to look for. This is specific bits of information you want macie to look
