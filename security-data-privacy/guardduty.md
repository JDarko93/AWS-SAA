## AWS GuardDuty

- Used as a thhreat detection tool
- It monitors activity related to S3 buckets, initiate malware scans in containers or EC2 instances, detect use of root account
- In the "findings" view. You can select what GuardDuty has found to futher look into the details such as resources it applies to etc
- Root credential usage gives you detailed breakdown of what happened
- Such as the severity, what has happened, the account ID it relates to
- Further details show the action, the actors IP address as well as location it was done from
